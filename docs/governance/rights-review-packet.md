## PyPI policy question

PyPI Support issue [#12999](https://github.com/pypi/support/issues/12999) remains open. On 2026-11-15 the project posted a factual update that FUSE 0.3.0 was published, that the tool is a defensive non-executing static analysis CLI, and that the owner authorized the 0.4.0 release. The comment explicitly does not infer PyPI approval: [issue comment](https://github.com/pypi/support/issues/12999#issuecomment-6895323988). No response from PyPI maintainers is recorded here.

The owner has authorized proceeding with 0.4.0 while project-specific policy guidance remains pending. Publication is not evidence of policy approval. The current PyPI Terms of Service describe the rights needed for PyPI distribution and mirrors; this license includes a narrow permission intended to cover official unmodified package releases. The project has not received confirmation from PyPI that its policy applies to FUSE. See the [PyPI Terms of Service](https://policies.python.org/pypi.org/Terms-of-Service/) and [Acceptable Use Policy](https://policies.python.org/pypi.org/Acceptable-Use-Policy/).
